JODE Launcher

A minimal launcher that does one thing well.

A clock, today’s screen time, your five most recent apps, and a search field that finds any installed app by name. No widgets, no folders, no news feed, no weather, no accounts, no backend.

It works entirely offline. The app has no internet permission and no analytics. Usage statistics are read on the device, with your explicit grant, only to order the recents row and total today’s screen time — and are never stored or sent anywhere.
